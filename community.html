Choice page 02 · people and place

Nothing good
happens alone.

The most meaningful thing I’ve learned is that community is not an audience. It is a practice of making room for each other.

Leave places better than you found them.

What I bring to a room

Curiosity,
care, and snacks.

I like being the person who asks the question everyone else was thinking, writes down what we decided, and remembers to make sure the quiet voice gets heard.

From student clubs to neighborhood events, I’ve found that the smallest acts of welcome change how people participate. Clear invitations, shared credit, and a little patience go a long way.

A few commitments

How I try
to show up.

01

Make the invitation specific.

Tell people what to expect, what they can bring, and why their perspective matters.

02

Share the microphone.

Good collaboration is not one person talking beautifully. It is many people making the work more true.

03

Follow up with action.

Say thank you, send the notes, and turn a good conversation into a next step.
