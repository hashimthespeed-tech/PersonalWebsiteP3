Choice page 02 · networking and travel

Better places
make better people.

Travel, sports, and networking keep me curious. Every new room is a chance to learn how someone else sees the world.

Every conversation can open a door.

What I bring to a room

Energy,
curiosity, and follow-through.

I like meeting ambitious people, asking questions, and finding the common ground that turns a quick introduction into a real relationship.

Sports have taught me how to compete and stay coachable. Travel reminds me there is always more to learn. Networking helps me connect those lessons to new opportunities.

A few commitments

How I try
to show up.

01

Make the invitation specific.

Tell people what to expect, what they can bring, and why their perspective matters.

02

Share the microphone.

Good collaboration is not one person talking beautifully. It is many people making the work more true.

03

Follow up with action.

Say thank you, send the notes, and turn a good conversation into a next step.
