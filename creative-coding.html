Choice page 01 · AI and opportunity

AI can be
a multiplier.

I’m interested in how artificial intelligence can help ambitious people learn faster, communicate better, and build opportunities that scale.

“Use technology to create leverage, not distance.”

A small manifesto

Build skills that compound.

AI is most exciting to me when it gives a person more room to think, connect, and act. I want to understand the tools deeply enough to use them responsibly and creatively.

I’m exploring ways AI can support business ideas, communication, learning, and passive income. The goal is not to skip the work — it is to make focused work go further.

How I work

Start with a
human question.

Tools are only useful when they help us notice, understand, or connect.

01 · Listen

Find the real question.

Before I open a code editor, I want to understand who this is for and what they already know.

02 · Prototype

Make the smallest version.

A rough, working thing teaches more than a polished idea trapped in a deck.

03 · Invite

Let other people shape it.

The best work has evidence of other people in it.
