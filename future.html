A direction, not a prediction

The next five
years are wide open.

I don’t want a perfectly straight line. I want a body of work that gets more generous, more useful, and more mine.

2026 → 2031

Grow slowly.
Go far.

Over the next five years, I hope to become the kind of creative technologist who can move between a sketchbook, a community room, and a production codebase without losing the thread: people deserve tools that respect their attention.

That means learning in public, working with people unlike me, and making enough small things that the big direction becomes clear through practice.

Specific goal

Build and launch an open-source neighborhood storytelling toolkit by 2031.

It will help local groups collect oral histories, publish them beautifully, and keep ownership of their stories. I’ll measure progress with a working pilot, three community partners, and documentation anyone can use.

The path I’m imagining

YEAR 01 — LEARN

Sharpen the fundamentals.

Deepen my front-end skills, practice interviewing, and keep a weekly habit of making and sharing.

YEAR 02 — CONTRIBUTE

Join work with a wider impact.

Collaborate with a small team building tools for education, culture, or local communities.

YEAR 03 — GATHER

Make space for more voices.

Host workshops and create a public archive of stories, experiments, and lessons learned.

YEAR 04 — BUILD

Turn the toolkit into a real pilot.

Work alongside partner organizations to test, revise, and make the work genuinely useful.

YEAR 05 — SHARE

Leave the door open.

Release the work openly, teach what I know, and start listening for what should come next.
