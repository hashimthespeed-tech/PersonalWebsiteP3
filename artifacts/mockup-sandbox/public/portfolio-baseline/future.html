A direction, not a prediction

The next five
years are wide open.

I don’t want a perfectly straight line. I want a body of work that gets more generous, more useful, and more mine.

2026 → 2031

Build smart.
Go far.

Over the next five years, I want to become an AI-fluent entrepreneur with strong communication skills, a meaningful network, and the discipline to turn good ideas into real income.

I want to keep improving through sports, travel, and hands-on projects — learning from people who are already where I want to go and putting that knowledge into action.

Specific goal

Reach $20,000 per month in passive income by 2031.

I’ll work toward that goal by building scalable AI-enabled products, learning how business and investing work, and developing repeatable systems that create value beyond my own hours.

The path I’m imagining

YEAR 01 — LEARN

Master the fundamentals.

Build stronger AI, sales, communication, and personal finance skills while staying disciplined in sports.

YEAR 02 — CONNECT

Build a serious network.

Meet founders, athletes, investors, and creators who expand my perspective and challenge me to grow.

YEAR 03 — BUILD

Launch useful AI projects.

Turn ideas into products and services that solve real problems and can grow beyond one client at a time.

YEAR 04 — SCALE

Create repeatable income.

Improve the strongest projects, document the systems, and reinvest in what is working.

YEAR 05 — COMPOUND

Reach the $20k monthly target.

Keep learning, keep traveling, and build a life with more freedom, flexibility, and impact.
